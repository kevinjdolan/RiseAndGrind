# Rise & Grind artwork

The app icon and hero art were generated locally through Codex's built-in `imagegen` tool; no stock artwork or third-party asset service was used.

## Production prompt

> Create a polished square iOS app icon for an app called Rise & Grind, with no words, letters, numbers, logos, watermark, border, or rounded-corner mask. Fuse a chrome twin-bell alarm clock with a glossy black espresso cup, a sharp business tie, an industrial gear, and an aggressively rising arrow. Make it an ironic maximalist hustle-culture emblem: premium 3D product rendering, centered and immediately readable at small size, dramatic sunrise rays, explosive energy, black and polished chrome with vivid gold, orange, and restrained hot-magenta accents. Keep the composition symmetrical enough for an app icon, fill the full square canvas, use an opaque background, crisp high-contrast silhouette, and avoid tiny UI details.
